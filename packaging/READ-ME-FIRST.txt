GUSH — granular tape echo / resonator / shimmer
================================================

  1. Right-click install.command  ->  Open  ->  Open.
     (Just double-clicking it will be blocked. This is unsigned software,
     which macOS treats with suspicion, correctly.)

  2. Open Ableton. Preferences -> Plug-Ins -> turn on VST3, and Audio Units
     if you prefer AU. Press Rescan.

  3. Drag GUSH onto an AUDIO track.

     GUSH is an EFFECT, not an instrument. Nothing happens on an empty MIDI
     track. Give it a guitar, the Digitakt, or an audio clip.

Want to hear it before installing anything? The demos folder has audio
rendered straight through this build. Play them in order.

No DAW at all? GUSH.app gets installed to your Applications folder. Open it,
pick your audio interface in its settings, and play.

Where to start on the knobs
---------------------------
  MIX             how much of any of this you hear
  ECHO MIX        the tape delay
  GRAIN BLEND     turn it up and the echoes break into grains
  RESONATE        the echoes strike a tuned object instead of a speaker
  REVERB, DECAY   the room
  SHIMMER         an octave up, climbing inside the reverb tail
  FREEZE          holds the loop forever while you keep playing over it

The interface is a plain list of controls for now. Everything works and
everything automates; it just has not been dressed yet.
